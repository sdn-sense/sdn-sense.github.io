## Fixes

- Docker startup selects the intended SiteRM image.
- Model generation no longer rewrites version information unexpectedly.
- Old models are cleaned in batches of ten to avoid memory pressure during large cleanup runs.

The release supports Frontend EL9, Agent EL8/EL9/EL10, and Debugger EL10.
