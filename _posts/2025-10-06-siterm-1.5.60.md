## What changed

- SENSE provides preconfigured VPP/FRR/DPDK router containers for Kubernetes deployments with NIC passthrough.
- RTMon can drive iPerf3, FDT, ETHR, ping, and traceroute through the SiteRM workflow from hosts and network devices.
- Junos collection moves to XML and reduces the documented configuration-query time from roughly two minutes to about 30 seconds.
- SiteRM validates VLAN use across ports and configured ranges to detect conflicts.
- Arista devices gain VRF support.
- The Frontend proxies Node Exporter, so sites do not need to expose the exporter port directly to central monitoring.
- Large Ansible results are stored in files instead of crossing subprocess and asyncio message-size limits.

The release also added VPP/DPDK monitoring, disk monitoring, and several model and LLDP handling fixes.
