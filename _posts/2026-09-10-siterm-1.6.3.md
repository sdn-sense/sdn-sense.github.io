## What changed

- Debugger adds `ethr-server` and `ethr-client`, while transfer-test servers can allocate ports from a configured range.
- Delta submit, commit, force-commit, and force-apply operations now accept `write` credentials in addition to `admin` credentials.
- SiteRM can optionally trust access tokens from a configured SENSE-O Keycloak realm at a fixed permission level.
- Service state reporting adds permanent numeric `exccode` values and a `siterm_error_events` Prometheus metric.
- Non-operational switch ports are excluded from the published model. Explicitly configured ports also raise `SWITCH_PORT_OPER_DOWN` (`-119`) until the port recovers.
- First-run provisioning checks live device facts before reapplying existing virtual-switch or single-port deltas.

## Upgrade notes

- The undocumented `ansible_params` key in the Git switch configuration is no longer read. Move per-switch settings to the prepared `host_vars` source in `/etc/ansible-conf.yaml` before upgrading.
- Review credential permissions if your deployment relied on delta submit or commit being restricted to administrators.

This release also fixes stale model warnings, several Debugger submission failures, temporary Ansible fact-file leaks, model cleanup safety, switchport detection, and first-run provisioning error handling.
